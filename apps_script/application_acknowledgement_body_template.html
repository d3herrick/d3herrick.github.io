Thank you for submitting an application to the Newton Tree Conservancy's Community Tree Planting program.

Your application indicated that you will be leading the tree planting group named in your neighborhood. In addition to contacting homeowners directly, you may download the Dear Neighbor Letter Template, enter the details of your group in the template, then email or leave a printed copy of the letter with them.

If by the application deadline for the next planting day the neighborhood planting group you specified in your application included at least 8 trees and 5 homeowners, the director from the Newton Tree Conservancy who is assigned to that planting group will be in touch with you as we approach the planting day. Otherwise, the Newton Tree Conservancy may include your application in a future planting, if a group forms near you.

Note: The Newton Tree Conservancy will install a watering bag on each tree you receive. You must agree to water those trees by filling their watering bags from mid-April to mid-November for two years. If you, or a family member, friend or neighbor are unable to fulfill this requirement, please inform the Newton Tree Conservancy in a reply to this email.
